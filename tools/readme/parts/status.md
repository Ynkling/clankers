## Status, machines and the Phase VI tests

**Revision 8 (7–10 October 2026).** Revision 8 added Phase VI: two pre-registered tests run on two machines with disjoint seeds, an audit of the project's records (`docs/audit_2026-10.md`), five exploratory batches on the side branch, three exploratory sessions run in parallel on their own branches, and a reading of twelve papers on context routing and memory (`docs/reading/`). Its first draft was reviewed by an independent referee session, which found statements that were wrong when committed; Revision 8.1 corrected them and nothing else (the list is in Section [15](#15-the-referees-report-and-the-claim-registry); the README's text as of Revision 8.1 is kept in `docs/revision8.md`).

REVISION9_PARAGRAPH

**Machines and sessions.** Two machines ran every main-line test: **X**, a cloud container on Intel Xeon hosts at 2.10 and 2.80 GHz, and **L**, an Intel i7-12650H laptop. From the Muon-recipe test on, X and L run disjoint seeds, and the pooled claim is primary. The exploratory sessions are **E** (branch `claude/outside-ideas`, batches 1–20), **F**, **G** and **H** (branches `claude/explore-F/G/H`: delta-rule channels, distant cues, and the split's devices and constants), **I** (branch `claude/explore-I`: a dense-loss task and a GPU package, run on the user's GTX 1660 SUPER and RTX 3070 Laptop GPU), **R** (branch `claude/replicate-R`: the clean-room replication) and **V** (branch `claude/review-V`, merged here under `docs/review_2026-10/`: the referee); F, G, H and R ran on 2.10 GHz Xeons. Every verdict was fixed before its run and printed mechanically.

**After Revision 9 (recorded by 14 October 2026; not yet in the report).** `test_delta_recipe` is complete on L too (`results/L/`; the docstring's RESULT (L) and RESULT (POOLED) blocks): pooled over 40 pairs, the delta recipe binds eight streams 35/40 against the Hebbian recipe's 35/40 (*d* = 0; "delta not shown worse by more than 2 pairs"), the split is NOT SHOWN on delta channels, where the no-trigger arm binds 38/40 (NO ROOM), and SHOWN on Hebbian channels (35/40 against 17/40, 19 vs 1, *p* = 2×10⁻⁵); the pre-fixed readings "delta: no room; Hebbian: helps" apply. Exploratory and replication results since the revision, which are not claims: session I ran `test_window_gate`'s eight-stream arms on a GPU with fresh seeds (the recipe 19/20, no split 6/20, the recurrent gate 0/20; branch `claude/explore-I`, report 3); session R replicated `test_split_copy`'s two results from the specification alone (18/20, 9/20 and 0/20, each inside the main line's interval; `claude/replicate-R`, Phase 5); session G found a hinge that reads no task labels, a frequency table of token ids, discovering the register 17/20 (`claude/explore-G`, S87); session H held the all-position trigger within two pairs of the key-position rule on 30 seeds (`claude/explore-H`, S87); session E found the sixteen-key runs stuck rather than slow (`claude/outside-ideas`, batch 20) and then that the perfect gate at *D* = 128 with sixteen keys binds only 4/10 under the recorded recipe and 0/10 under SLOW, so the drafted key-load test has no validity check at its budget (batch 21); S75's last arm closed that screen at 0/10 and its follow-up, S75b, was prepared and not run; session F's last screen, S88, found that a learned per-token write strength on delta channels collapses to zero and binds 7/10 against the fixed strength's 10/10 ("no reading applies" under its pre-fixed rules) and that its horizon screen was untested, the learned forget gate binding 6/10 at the shorter distance (`claude/explore-F`, report 8). [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) has every thread and the plan; these results go into Revision 10.
