### tex:205/384 in detail: does the second draft say it correctly?

Yes. Every number reproduces from `results/{X,L}/window_gate_results.json` at the head (`check_appendixB_v2.md` §3).

- **The probe reading is stored.** `checks[*].acc` holds it (twg:527 `row = dict(step=step, acc=acc, prev=..., map_before=cmap, key_masses, all_masses, ...)`, appended at twg:546). The curve holds the evaluation at the same update.
- **Per-stream masses are stored.** `stats[*].stream_gate` holds them at every evaluation, and every check step is an evaluation step. This makes tex2:344's "(they store both)" true.
- **6 rows.** Six rows read the probe ≥ 0.95 while the evaluation read < 0.95 (evaluation range 0.8652–0.8804, so the draft's "0.865–0.88" is right):
  - X s552@24000
  - L s1550@14400
  - L s1553@16800 and @19200
  - L s1554@19200 and @21600
- **2 blocked by the probe alone.** In two of the six, the probe alone blocked a split: neither the gap nor the cap blocked it, and the probe rose < 0.02.
  - L s1553@19200: probe 0.953, evaluation 0.867. The next check, at 21600, fired.
  - L s1554@21600: probe 0.969, evaluation 0.869. The next check, at 24000, fired.
  - Both runs ended bound routed.
- **The reset arm had none:** 0 of its 145 rows.
- **Wording point ("393 checks").** 40 of the 393 rows are the 2400 reference readings. The draft's own definition (tex2:122, tex2:531) does not call these checks. By that definition the statement is "6 of 353 checks" or "6 of 393 probe readings". The count 6 is unaffected. The wording comes from my own A1 ("6 of 393 WIN3_SPLIT_D8 checks"), so the fix belongs in revision_9_check.md as much as in the draft.
