# Code of conduct

This is a small research repository. Everyone who takes part in it — in issues, pull requests,
reviews or discussion — is expected to be straightforward and courteous: argue about records, code
and text, not about people; quote what you disagree with; say what you checked and what you did not.
Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind are not acceptable here. The repository
owner handles reports (open an issue, or contact the owner through their GitHub profile) and may
remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this, and may block repeat
offenders.
